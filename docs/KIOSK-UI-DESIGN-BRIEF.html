Photobooth Kiosk

UI design brief — guest booth experience, mobile share page, and SumUp payment variations.

1. Project summary

Guests use a branded fullscreen web app on a portrait kiosk. They consent, optionally pay via SumUp, choose an AI “look”, take up to three photos (each is AI-styled immediately), pick their favourite, then get a dedicated screen with QR / download / print — not mixed with the large preview image.

Design goals
  • Touch-first, large targets, readable at arm’s length
  • Clear step-by-step flow; minimal text; strong primary CTA per screen
  • Per-tenant branding: primary colour, secondary background, optional logo
  • Portrait 2:3 photo areas consistent with 6×4″ print output
